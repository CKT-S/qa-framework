# Submit a Question

Use this form to submit a question for the Ask Dr. Sitara Q&A series.

Please share enough context for the question to be understood, but avoid including real names, exact locations, workplace details, contact information, or anything that could identify another person.

If selected, your question may be edited, condensed, anonymized, or reframed for clarity before publication. The purpose is to protect your privacy, preserve the heart of your question, and make the answer useful to a broader audience.

## Submission Note

Submitting a question does not guarantee publication or a personal response. This Q&A is educational and reflective, not therapy or emergency support. If you are in crisis, unsafe, or dealing with coercion, abuse, or severe emotional distress, please seek support from a qualified professional or emergency resource in your area.
